## 2. Platform-by-Platform Breakdown

### 2.1 LinkedIn — The B2B Client Engine (100% Work)
- **What belongs here:**
  - Case studies of apps you shipped (RealtorOne, Skoolwala, WeSync, CareOnDemand).
  - Architecture breakdowns: PostgreSQL row-level security, Flutter state management, Traefik SSL.
  - Client wins and delivery milestones.
  - "Trader | Full-Stack Developer" headline.
- **What DOES NOT belong here:**
  - Gaming clips, guitar memes, casual unboxing. Keep LinkedIn strictly high-value so founders see you as a top-tier contractor.

### 2.2 YouTube — The Multi-Pillar Media Hub (Organized by Playlists)
On YouTube, you can do **everything you love**—gaming, guitar, tech unboxing, trading, AND building software—by structuring your channel with **clear playlists**:

1. **Playlist 1: "Building Software & SaaS in Public" (THE #1 MONEY MAKER)**
   - *Should you make software videos?* **ABSOLUTELY YES.**
   - Screen record yourself building a real app from GitHub, explaining how the backend works, or deploying to a VPS.
   - These videos get the highest sponsorship rates (RPM $15–$30) and attract founders who will DM you to hire you for $5k–$20k contracts.
2. **Playlist 2: "Trading & TradeShield"**
   - Market reviews, how you code MQL5 / Pine Script tools, trading psychology.
   - Attracts traders who purchase custom indicators and automation tools.
3. **Playlist 3: "Tech, Gadgets & Desk Setup"**
   - Unboxing new gear, monitors, mics, audio interfaces, and tech tools.
   - High search volume + Amazon/affiliate marketing revenue.
4. **Playlist 4: "Guitar & Gaming"**
   - Casual streams, game highlights, guitar riffs, and acoustic sessions.
   - Builds genuine fan loyalty and personal connection.

### 2.3 Twitter / X — The Real-Time Builder (80% Tech + 20% Life)
- **What belongs here:**
  - 45s screen recordings of your GitHub apps running smoothly.
  - Thoughts on new AI models (Claude, Gemini, OpenAI) and modern dev stacks.
  - Occasional photos of your desk setup, new gadgets bought, or a quick market trade chart.
- **Why it works:** Twitter is where tech founders hang out and hire developers directly through DMs.

### 2.4 Instagram (@yasir_subhani — 24.8K Followers) — The Lifestyle & Proof
- **What belongs here:**
  - **Reels:** 30–60s fast coding timelapses, clean app UI animations, guitar riffs, trading setups.
  - **Stories:** Daily work in progress, new gadgets unboxed, gaming moments.
  - **Highlights (Already organized!):**
    - ✈️ Travel
    - 🚘 Cars / Life
    - 📝 Code / Work
    - 💹 Trading
    - 🎶🎸 Guitar & Music
    - 🎮 Gaming (create a new highlight!)
