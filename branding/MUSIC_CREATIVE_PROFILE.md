## 1. The Intersection of Sound & Software

To the outside world, software engineering and music production seem like opposites. In reality, they are cousins:
- **Modular Synthesis is Architecture:** Oscillators are data sources, filters are transformation pipelines, envelopes are state lifecycles, and mixing is optimization.
- **Rhythm is Algorithmic:** Tempo, syncopation, and polyrhythms demand rigorous mathematical precision.
- **Atmospheric Immersion:** Just as great software feels effortless and intuitive, great ambient and electronic music creates an immersive flow state.
